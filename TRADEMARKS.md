# Kortext Trademark Guidelines & Brand Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

This document outlines the trademark policy and branding guidelines for the **Kortext** open-source project. The purpose of these guidelines is to protect the integrity of the project, provide clarity for commercial and non-commercial users, and encourage vibrant community participation while avoiding confusion regarding official endorsement or origin.

---

## 1. The Kortext Trademark

The names **Kortext**, the Kortext wordmark, and the official Kortext logos (including all stylized variations in `assets/logo.svg` and `assets/logo.png`) are trademarks of Supreme Knight and the Kortext maintainers.

While the software itself is licensed under the copyleft terms of the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)**, trademark rights are distinct from copyright licenses. The GPL-3.0 gives you the right to modify and distribute the source code, but does not grant rights to use the trademarks, logos, or brand identifiers in a manner that creates confusion about the origin of software or services.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Explicit Permission Needed)

You may use the Kortext wordmark without prior written permission in the following circumstances:

### A. Accurate Factual Attribution & Compatibility
You may use the wordmark to truthfully and accurately describe software, tools, or integrations that work with Kortext, provided that:
- You state clearly that your product is *compatible with*, *built for*, or *integrates with* Kortext.
- Examples of acceptable phrasing:
  - *"Plugin for Kortext"*
  - *"An Antigravity extension compatible with Kortext"*
  - *"Python client designed for Kortext FastMCP servers"*
- Examples of **unacceptable** phrasing:
  - *"Kortext Enterprise"* (implies official release)
  - *"Official Kortext Plugin"* (implies maintainer endorsement)
  - *"The Kortext Platform"* (creates brand collision)

### B. Educational, Academic, and Technical Content
You may use the name in technical articles, tutorials, books, research papers, and academic presentations analyzing or explaining Kortext architecture (such as the 5-tier memory model, Tree-sitter AST extraction, or Task DAG execution).

### C. Linking and Distribution
You may link to the official repository (`https://github.com/bravest-cat/cortex`) and mention that your project depends on or communicates with Kortext.

---

## 3. Uses Requiring Prior Written Approval

You may **not** do any of the following without explicit, written permission from the project maintainers:

1. **Naming Products or Services:** You may not name your company, domain name, commercial service, SaaS offering, or product using "Kortext" as the leading or primary identifier (e.g., `kortext-cloud.io`, `Kortext Pro`).
2. **Altered or Modified Logos:** You may not modify, distort, recolor, or disassemble the official logo or combine it with other marks to create a composite logo.
3. **Forks and Derivative Works:**
   - If you distribute a modified version or hard fork of Kortext under the GPL-3.0, you **must remove or rename** official branding and logo assets unless the modifications are purely trivial bug fixes submitted upstream.
   - Derivative works and hard forks should adopt a distinct name (e.g., *"MyProject, derived from Kortext"*).
4. **Endorsement Claims:** You may not state or imply that your organization, plugin, or consultancy is sponsored, authorized, certified, or endorsed by Kortext or its core maintainers.

---

## 4. Logo Usage Specifications

When using official Kortext brand assets:
- **Aspect Ratio:** Maintain the original 1:1 or vector proportions; do not stretch or squish.
- **Clear Space:** Maintain a clear margin around the mark equal to at least 25% of the mark's diameter.
- **Contrast:** Ensure the mark is rendered with strong contrast against background surfaces (recommended on dark slate `#020617` or pure dark themes).
- **Format:** Prefer the scalable vector asset `assets/logo.svg` for digital web and application rendering.
